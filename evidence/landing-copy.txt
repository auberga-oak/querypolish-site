## What the audit covers 
An AI model can't mention a business it hasn't read. The same goes for Google. The audit looks at the website you already have and asks three questions.  
- Read. Can Google, and the crawlers that AI tools send, see every page you already have? 
- Found. When a customer asks a question, does the right page come up? Two of your pages shouldn't compete for the same question. 
- Mentioned. Do the answers name your business and link to your site? Where an answer is wrong, the fix belongs on your site, not in the chat.  
It also covers:  
- Your Google Business Profile: categories, attributes, services, description, photos and posts, set against what your competitors do. If you don't have a profile yet, the report starts with how to set one up. 
- Your Google reviews (if you have a profile): how many you have, how often new ones come in, what customers mention, and how your competitors' reviews differ. 
- Your website: whether your main service pages match the searches your customers use, and which topics your competitors cover that you don't. 
- Your business details: whether your name, address and phone number match across the web. For online-only businesses, we check your profiles on directories, social sites and marketplaces instead.  
Each finding comes with a suggested fix and a note on how much it's likely to matter.   

## How it works  
- Fill in the form below. You only need to give us your website and your email address. 
- We look at public information: your profile if you have one, your website, and your closest competitors. We also put the questions your customers ask to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google. 
- A person reads the report first. Then we email it to the address you give us.  
We never ask for passwords or logins. The audit uses only what you tell us and what anyone can see on Google and on the web. We only ask for access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics if you say yes to more work after the report.   

## Privacy 
We use what you send only to prepare your audit and email it to you. We don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. You can ask us to delete it at any time. The privacy note has the details. This page stores nothing. We only receive what you send in the form.
