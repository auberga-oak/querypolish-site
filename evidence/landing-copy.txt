# A free audit of how your business shows up in search 
Tell us your website. We compare your Google Business Profile, if you have one, and your website with your closest competitors for the searches your customers use, and email you a written report. 
Request the audit   

## What the audit covers  
- Your Google Business Profile, if we can find one: categories, attributes, services, description, photos and posts, set against what your competitors do. If you don't have a profile yet, the report starts with how to set one up. 
- Your Google reviews (if you have a profile): how many you have, how often new ones come in, what customers mention, and how your competitors' reviews differ. 
- Your website: whether your main service pages match the searches your customers use, and which topics your competitors cover that you don't. 
- Your business details: whether your name, address and phone number match across the web. For online-only businesses, we check your profiles on directories, social sites and marketplaces instead.  
Each finding comes with a suggested fix and a note on how much it's likely to matter.   

## How it works  
- Fill in the form below. You only need to give us your website and your email address. 
- We look at public information: your profile if you have one, your website, and your closest competitors. 
- We email the report to the address you give us.  
We never ask for passwords or logins. The audit uses only what you tell us and what anyone can see on Google and on the web.   

## Privacy 
We use what you send only to prepare your audit and email it to you. We don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. You can ask us to delete it at any time. The privacy note has the details.

# Privacy 
Who is responsible. Finansium AB is the data controller for Query Polish. Contact: henrik.ekenberg@finansium.se. 
What we collect. What you type into the audit form: your website address, your email address, the language you want the report in, and anything else you choose to tell us. We never ask for passwords or logins. 
Why. Only to prepare your audit and email the report to you. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. We don't sell your information, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. 
Who processes it. Web3Forms (web3forms.com) receives the form and forwards it to us by email, as our data processor. 
How long. We keep your answers and the report for 12 months after we send it, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner. 
Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing the address above. If you think we've handled it wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.
