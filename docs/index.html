What the audit covers

  • Your Google Business Profile: categories, attributes, services, description, photos and posts, set against what your competitors do.
  • Your reviews: how many you have, how often new ones come in, what customers mention, and how your competitors' reviews differ.
  • Your website: whether your main service pages match the searches you named, and which local topics your competitors cover that you don't.
  • Your business details: whether your name, address and phone number match across the web.

Each finding comes with a suggested fix and a note on how much it's likely to matter.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below. It takes about ten minutes. Only the fields marked with a star are required.
  2. We look at public information: your profile, your website, and the three competitors you name.
  3. We email the report to the address you give us.

We never ask for passwords or logins. The audit uses only what you tell us and what anyone can see on Google and on the web.

Privacy

We use what you send only to prepare your audit and email it to you. We don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. You can ask us to delete it at any time. The privacy note has the details.
