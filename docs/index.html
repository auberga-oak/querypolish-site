What the audit covers

Google and AI tools answer from what they can read. The audit looks at the website you already have and checks three things.

  1. Read. Can Google, and the crawlers that AI tools send, see every page you already have?
  2. Found. When a customer searches, does the right page come up on Google? Two of your pages shouldn't compete for the same search.
  3. Ready to mention. Does each page say plainly, in text, who you are, what you do and where? We can't control what an AI tool says, but we can make your site easy to read and quote.

It also covers:

  • Your Google Business Profile, if we can find one: categories, attributes, services, description, photos and posts, set against what your competitors do. If you don't have a profile yet, the report starts with how to set one up.
  • Your Google reviews (if you have a profile): how many you have, how often new ones come in, what customers mention, and how your competitors' reviews differ.
  • Your website: whether your main service pages match the searches your customers use, and which topics your competitors cover that you don't.
  • Your business details: whether your name, address and phone number match across the web. For online-only businesses, we check your profiles on directories, social sites and marketplaces instead.

Each finding comes with a suggested fix and a note on how much it's likely to matter.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below. You only need to give us your website and your email address.

We never ask for passwords or logins. The audit uses only what you tell us and what anyone can see on Google and on the web. If you want more work after the report, we may ask you to add us as a user in Google Search Console and Google Analytics. You never share a password.
